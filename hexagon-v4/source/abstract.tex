We classify null-projected supersymmetric Yang--Mills theories with a Majorana--Weyl gaugino in signature $(s,t)$, defined by five conditions on the gaugino constraint, the transformation rules, the residual gauge field, the translation bracket and the absorption of the cubic gaugino terms. Such a theory built on $k$ null vectors exists if and only if $s+t-2k=10$ and $s-t\in\{8,0,-8\}$, or $s+t-2k=2$ and $s=t$. The key step is a converse of the cubic gamma-matrix identity: symmetric gamma matrices that satisfy it act on spinors of dimension $2(d-2)$. Hence the signatures $(9+8n+q)+(1+q)$ with $n\ge1$ and $0\le q\le3$ proposed by Rios, Marrani and Chester, among them $27+3$, carry no such theory, and neither another contraction of the source in the gauge field equation nor several gauginos with their own parameters change this. In the single-time signatures $(9+8n)+1$ no unitary massless multiplet without tensor charges consists of scalars, spinors and vectors.
