Please consider "Residual-dimension obstructions for null-projected supersymmetric Yang--Mills theories" by Deep Bhattacharjee.

The paper studies a specific proposed extension of Nishino's null-projection mechanism. Under five explicit assumptions on a full-corner Majorana--Weyl gaugino, its transformations, a nontrivial translation bracket and absorption of the quadratic gaugino variation, the residual dimension must be two or ten. Together with the real Witt-index bound, this forces the signature difference to be zero or eight in absolute value. It obstructs the stated transfer to families including nineteen plus three and twenty-seven plus three. It does not rule out all possible theories in those signatures or construct theories in every signature left by these necessary conditions.

The main algebraic step derives the cubic gamma identity's dimension restriction by two explicit contractions. The revision constructs the residual vector structure and the fermionic source current directly. A separate unitary single-time argument supplies a helicity-span bound, closing the gap between excluding one vector multiplet by state counting and excluding arbitrary finite multiplicities of scalar, spinor and vector fields. All general arguments are written in the text. Thirty-four fresh explicit gamma-matrix checks passed; these are supplementary floating-point diagnostics, not a proof of the general theorem.

The manuscript compares its assumptions and conclusions directly with Nishino's published equations and the published proposal of Rios, Marrani and Chester. The preceding preprint is https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00471v1; the original source and numerical programs are available at https://github.com/creelie/cs-grav and the earlier archive https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23558751. The present manuscript revision is archived at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23611588; its source and verification files are at https://github.com/creelie/Paper_4. It adds proof details and explicit scope corrections. AI assistance is disclosed.
