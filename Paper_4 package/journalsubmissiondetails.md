## Disclosure and scope

The paper proves necessary residual-dimension and signature conditions for null-projected supersymmetric Yang-Mills constructions under five explicit axioms. It also gives a separate unitary helicity-span obstruction. It does not assert existence in every allowed signature or a nonperturbative quantum construction.

Formal-check scope: No general Lean formalisation is claimed. The proof is written in the manuscript; the gamma-matrix calculations are supplementary finite numerical checks.
