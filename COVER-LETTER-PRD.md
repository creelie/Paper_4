Please consider "Residual-dimension obstructions for null-projected supersymmetric Yang–Mills theories" by Deep Bhattacharjee.

The manuscript proves necessary restrictions on a precise null-projected Yang–Mills construction under five explicit assumptions N1–N5. Majorana reality bounds the null-frame rank by the Witt index; projection of the absorption equation produces a residual vector pairing and cubic gamma identity. Direct contractions restrict the residual dimension to two or ten and the signature difference to zero or eight in absolute value. A separate unitary massless argument derives a helicity-span bound that excludes arbitrary finite multiplicities of low-spin polarizations in the stated higher-dimensional single-time family.

The work concerns supersymmetric gauge theory and spacetime symmetry. Its general necessary-condition theorem is presented as a Regular Article, rather than as a short Comment on one proposed model.

The analysis compares the stated transfer of Nishino's mechanism with the published equations of Nishino and the proposal of Rios, Marrani and Chester. It does not exclude every conceivable theory in the discussed signatures, prove sufficiency in every allowed signature or construct a nonperturbative quantum model. The general arguments are written in full; 34 numerical gamma-matrix checks are supplementary diagnostics. AI assistance and its substantive roles are disclosed.
